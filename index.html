How to Play

Goal: destroy the enemy Command Base. Lose yours and the war is over.

Credits trickle in from your base and from every Relay Tower you hold. Park units next to a tower to capture it (Engineers capture 3× faster). Kills pay a bounty too.

Build units from the bar at the bottom. Hold or right-click a button to cancel one. Tap your base, then the map, to set a rally point.

Cards: every so often (and every 6 kills) you draw a tactical card. Tap one to use it; some need a target on the map.

Veterans: units rank up after 3, 7 and 12 kills, gaining damage and armor.

Touch

  • Tap a unit to select it. Double-tap selects every unit of that type on screen.
  • With units selected, tap the ground to move or tap an enemy to attack.
  • Drag with one finger to scroll. Pinch to zoom. Turn on BOX to drag-select instead.
  • A-MOVE: units attack anything they meet on the way.
  • Tap the minimap to jump there.

Mouse & keyboard

  • Left-drag to box-select, left or right click to order, middle-drag or arrow keys to scroll, wheel to zoom.
  • A attack-move · S stop · B box mode · Ctrl+A select all · Esc cancel · 1–7 build · Z X C V (Shift+B) play cards.
